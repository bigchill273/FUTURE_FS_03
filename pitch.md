### Website Proposal for Blaze Addis

**Blaze Addis Café, Restaurant & Bakery** is a food business located in Megenagna, Addis Ababa, offering a variety of fast foods, traditional Ethiopian dishes, freshly baked pastries, and catering services for events.

The proposed website aims to solve the problem of limited online visibility and the difficulty customers may face when exploring the business's menu, discovering available products, and placing orders. By providing a well-organized and user-friendly online platform, customers can easily browse food categories, view products and prices, and learn about the services offered.

The website will help attract more customers through an attractive, mobile-friendly design, high-quality food photography, an interactive menu, and convenient ordering through Telegram. It will also showcase catering services and special offerings, helping potential customers discover Blaze Addis, build trust in the business, and make purchasing decisions more easily.
